Thermal Vision. Reimagined.

IRIS converts Landsat thermal surface-temperature imagery into an AI-generated plausible RGB reconstruction using a Pix2Pix-based deep learning pipeline.

01

MISSION

From thermal observations to visual interpretation

A single thermal band cannot uniquely recover the original visible scene. IRIS therefore produces a plausible RGB reconstruction learned from paired Landsat observations.

01

Thermal Input

Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 ST_B10 is converted from digital numbers into surface temperature before model inference.

02

Quality Masking

Optional QA_PIXEL masking removes cloud, cirrus, shadow, snow and invalid pixels before selecting the cleanest inference region.

03

Validation

Matching B2, B3 and B4 bands can optionally be supplied to calculate L1, PSNR and SSIM against visible-band ground truth.

TECHNOLOGY

INPUT

Surface Temperature

MODEL

Conditional GAN

EVALUATION

Quantitative Validation

When matching visible bands are supplied, reconstructed pixels are compared with the corresponding RGB target.
